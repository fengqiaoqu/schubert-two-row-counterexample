# Scope, provenance, and reuse

This repository presents a mathematical note and exact computational certificates for an infinite family of counterexamples to Conjecture 1.3 in Tao–Zheng, arXiv:2411.19735v1.

## What is and is not claimed

- The note gives a proof for every integer $m\ge6$. Finite computational checks are supporting evidence and do not replace that proof.
- The refuted statement is the linear upper bound **as printed** in the cited version. The general problem of an unsigned combinatorial rule for arbitrary Schubert structure constants remains unresolved by this work.
- First discovery, priority, external peer review, author endorsement, and formal proof-assistant verification have **not** been established.
- Internal proof reviews and code checks used OpenAI Codex and collaborating AI agents. They are not external human peer review. AI assistance was used in research, mathematical drafting, programming, translation, and preparation of this repository.
- The classical formulas and correspondences used in the proof are established mathematics; the note does not claim to originate them.
