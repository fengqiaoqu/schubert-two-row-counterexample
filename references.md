# References and source scope

## Conjecture being refuted

Zijie Tao and Yunchi Zheng, *Bounds On Schubert Coefficients in the Two-Row Case*, arXiv:2411.19735v1 (2024), 13 pages.

- [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19735).
- [Version-pinned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.19735v1).
- [Version-pinned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.19735v1).
- The target is **Conjecture 1.3, PDF page 2**: the two-row coefficient is bounded above by $n_2-k$ under the stated Grassmannian hypotheses.
- The additional hypotheses in Theorem 1.2 are not part of Conjecture 1.3. The counterexample concerns the latter conjecture, not a claimed disproof of Theorem 1.2.
- The discussion following Remark 4.5 and Example 4.6, PDF page 13, motivates the conjecture by computations. The PDF and HTML agree on the linear bound.

The arXiv submission record lists v1 as submitted on 2024-11-29; the PDF has an internal date of 2024-12-02. Both identify the same version. Source status was checked on 2026-10-22. No prior published refutation or correction was located in the checks performed for this note; this is not a proof of priority or of the absence of unpublished knowledge.

## Classical tools used in the proof

The note uses the standard Grassmannian–Schur correspondence, Littlewood–Richardson rule, two-row Jacobi–Trudi identity, Pieri rule, and ballot-word reflection argument. These classical results are not claimed as new. The cited preprint's Remark 2.6 and Lemmas 2.8–2.9 record the correspondence, Jacobi–Trudi identity, and Schubert Pieri formula relevant to its setting.

For a standard systematic reference: William Fulton, *Young Tableaux: With Applications to Representation Theory and Geometry*, London Mathematical Society Student Texts 35, Cambridge University Press, 1997. DOI: [10.1017/CBO9780511626241](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511626241).
